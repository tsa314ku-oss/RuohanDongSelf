Functional Wound Dressing

02 / BIOMATERIALS & WOUND CARE

2.Development of Functional Hemostatic Band-Aids Based on Composite Natural Repair Factors from Capsella bursa-pastoris Extract

Early-stage planning

Our team is developing a novel multifunctional hemostatic band‑aid using shepherd’s‑purse natural extract and chitosan‑silver nanomulsion. Targeting drawbacks of ordinary band‑aids with weak antibacterial and repair capabilities, our design integrates hemostasis, infection inhibition and wound‑healing promotion. Currently this project stays in the early planning phase. We have completed literature research and outlined experimental schemes, including plant extraction, material formulation optimization, in‑vitro cell tests and subsequent rodent animal validation, to produce a prototype of nature‑derived wound‑care dressing.
