Major Courses

1.Biochemistry

Biochemistry focuses on biomacromolecules, enzyme kinetics, metabolism and molecular genetic mechanisms. It lays theoretical foundations for biomedical and biological‑material research.

2.Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics

This course introduces fundamental theories of probability, random variables, probability distributions, parameter estimation, hypothesis testing, regression and correlation analysis. It equips learners with tools to process experimental data, interpret statistical results and conduct quantitative analysis. It provides critical quantitative‑thinking support for processing experimental measurement data in biological and biomedical research.

3.Analytical Chemistry

This course introduces core principles and practical skills of chemical quantitative and qualitative analysis. It covers titration analysis, spectrophotometry, separation techniques, and basic instrumental analysis. Students learn to measure substance content, process experimental data, evaluate detection accuracy and precision. It supplies essential theoretical and hands‑on support for compound extraction, sample detection and quantitative measurement in biomedical laboratory research.

Key Courses

1.Basic Writing

This course aims to enhance students' basic skills in academic English writing such as genre writing and text analysis, to cultivate contemporary university students with international competitiveness, and to assist students in their future cross-cultural communication and learning.

2.Writing and the Environment

This interdisciplinary general‑education course combines academic writing practice with environmental‑related topics. It trains students to read environmental documents, collect literature materials, organize logical arguments, and complete expository, argumentative and research‑oriented writing assignments. The course improves critical thinking, academic expression and document‑writing competence. These skills support composing project reports, research summaries and popular‑science texts for scientific‑research work.
