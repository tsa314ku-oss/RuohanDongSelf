Campus Singer

Campus Top Ten Singer

I competed in the Campus Singer Competition of BUCT in 2025 and was honored as one of the Campus Top Ten Singers. Since then, I have been invited three times to serve as a judge for the Campus Singer Competition at China University of Petroleum, Beijing. I have also received invitations to perform at various campus events including the Maple Leaf Art Festival.
